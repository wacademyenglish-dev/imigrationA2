GRAMMAR BLUEPRINT

Talking about Travel Plans ✈️

1. GOING TO

Intentions & Plans

Use this when you decided to do something, but maybe it's not 100% booked yet.

"I am going to visit Paris."

2. PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Fixed Arrangements

Use this for 100% confirmed plans, like flights or hotel reservations.

"I am flying to Paris tomorrow."

TEACHER: "Are you going to travel soon? Do you have any flights booked?"

Student Starter "Yes, I am going to travel to... I am flying on..."
